Mofy is a personal media tracker for movies and TV shows.

Features:
- Browse and search a local IMDb-based catalog with semantic search
- Track your watch history and personal library
- On-device facet classification (genre, mood, rating, era) — no cloud required
- Watch Together: synchronized playback over WebRTC with friends
- Plays local files via libVLC

All processing happens on-device. No accounts, no telemetry, no ads.
